How to split expenses with roommates

Add everyone sharing the home, enter each bill and its payer, then choose who should share that expense. Rent starts as an equal split, but you can switch to custom dollar amounts when bedrooms or arrangements differ. Press Calculate roommate split to see each person's share, what they already paid, and the smallest practical set of reimbursements.

Example: different rent amounts, equal utilities

Three roommates share $2,400 rent. Alex has the largest bedroom and pays $1,000 in rent; Sam and Jordan each owe $700. Their $150 electricity bill is split equally, adding $50 to each person's share. The calculator accounts for who actually paid both bills before showing who should reimburse whom.

Roommate bill splitting FAQs

Can roommates pay different amounts of rent?

Yes. Choose Custom amounts for rent and enter each roommate's agreed share. The shares must add up to the full rent amount.

Can we split utilities equally but rent unequally?

Yes. Every expense has its own split settings. Use custom amounts for rent and equal split for electricity, water, internet, or other shared bills.

What if only two roommates share a grocery purchase?

For that expense, keep Equal split and select only the roommates who should share it.

Does the calculator track who paid each bill?

Yes. Select a payer for each expense. Results show everyone's fair share, payments made, and who owes whom.

Do we need an account?

No. Calculations happen in your browser. The page does not save a recurring household history, so copy your summary before leaving.
