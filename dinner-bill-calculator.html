One receipt, different drink orders? Enter the totals, choose who had alcohol, and get each person's share. No sign-up or receipt upload.

1. Enter the restaurant receipt

Include any tip already paid in the total above and enter it in the tip field for allocation. Don't add it twice. Food subtotal is calculated as total minus alcohol, tax and tip. For now, food is split equally by everyone.

2. Who was at dinner?

Check “Had alcohol” only for people sharing the alcohol subtotal. Everyone shares the food subtotal equally.

3. Choose how to split tax and tip

Proportional is a practical default for a combined restaurant receipt. If food and alcohol have different tax rates, this simplified calculation won't reproduce the exact category-specific taxes.

How does the calculation work?

Food is shared equally among all diners. Alcohol is shared equally only among checked drinkers. The tax and tip totals are then split either proportionally to those pretax shares or equally, depending on your selection. Amounts are rounded to cents while preserving the receipt total.

This calculator handles a single restaurant receipt and shows what each person owes. If different people paid separate expenses, use the main group expense calculator to settle who pays whom.
