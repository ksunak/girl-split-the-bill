DINNER BILL SPLITTING GUIDE

How to Split a Dinner Bill Fairly (Without the Awkward Math)

One check, different orders, shared appetizers, and maybe a birthday guest who shouldn't pay. Here's how to make the split fair without turning dinner into a spreadsheet session.

Try the free bill splitter →

1. Decide whether an equal split makes sense

If everyone ordered roughly the same amount, dividing the total evenly is usually easiest. But when one person orders a salad and another orders several courses, an equal split may feel unfair. Agree on the approach before calculating.

Example: A $180 dinner shared equally by four people is $45 each, assuming the $180 already includes tax and tip.

2. Split shared dishes only among the people who had them

Did three friends share appetizers while the fourth skipped them? Treat the appetizers as a separate expense shared by those three. Keep individual meals separate when needed.

In GirlSplitTheBill, you can add each expense and uncheck anyone who didn't participate.

3. Don't forget tax and tip

When people pay different amounts, allocate tax and tip in a way the group agrees is fair. One approach is proportional allocation: someone responsible for 25% of the pretax bill also pays 25% of shared tax and tip. For an equal split, simply divide the total including tax and tip.

The calculator lets you enter an expense amount and an additional tip. For proportional splits, calculate each person's amount and use Custom shares.

4. Cover the birthday guest

When everyone wants to treat the birthday person, include them in the dinner expense, then use Are you treating someone? to assign contributions toward their share.

Example: Five friends have a $250 dinner. Each person's initial share is $50. If the other four cover the birthday guest's full share equally, each contributes an additional $12.50.

5. Settle up without extra back-and-forth

For groups with multiple expenses and different payers, the calculator nets what everyone owes and is owed to reduce unnecessary transfers. Copy or share the final payment instructions with the group.
