WHY I BUILT THIS

Hi, I’m a finance girly who hates overcomplication. 💕

I built Girl Split the Bill because splitting a bill shouldn’t require another app, another account, or another password.

I just want the math handled.

I don’t want to hand over my email, contacts, or personal information just to figure out who owes whom after a trip, dinner, or lunch.

Because real trips and dinners aren't always split evenly. Someone skipped dinner. Someone else paid for the Airbnb. A few people went out for drinks. And sometimes we're all treating the birthday girl.

Girl Split the Bill is built for real-life splits like these.

Add what everyone spent, choose who was actually part of each expense, get the calculation, share it with the group, and move on.

No sign-up. No complicated spreadsheet. No unnecessary personal information.Just a simple calculation when you need it.

Settle up and get back to the good part — or move on from the trip altogether. 💕

— A fellow finance girly
